Detailed Step-by-Step Guide to Building a Small Language Model for
Business Insights
Step 1: Define Scope and Objectives
Objective: To build a small language model that gathers data, draws insights, and offers private
intelligence to companies, driving significant changes by increasing revenue and decreasing
costs.
Example Use Case: Analyzing customer feedback and sales data to identify trends and
opportunities.
Step 2: Identify Data Sources
Internal Data Sources:
• CRM Data: Customer demographics, purchase history, feedback.
• Sales Data: Transaction records, sales reports, product performance.
• Operational Data: Inventory levels, supply chain metrics, production efficiency.
• Financial Data: Revenue, costs, profit margins.
External Data Sources:
• Market Trends: Industry reports, competitor analysis.
• Economic Indicators: Inflation rates, GDP growth, consumer confidence indices.
• Regulatory Data: Compliance requirements, industry standards.

Hierarchical Integration:
import torch.nn as nn
class HierarchicalModel(nn.Module):
def __init__(self, high_level_model, low_level_model):
super(HierarchicalModel, self).__init__()
self.high_level_model = high_level_model
self.low_level_model = low_level_model
self.fc =
nn.Linear(high_level_model.config.hidden_size +
low_level_model.config.hidden_size, output_size)
def forward(self, input_ids, attention_mask):
low_level_outputs =
self.low_level_model(input_ids=input_ids,
attention_mask=attention_mask)
low_level_features =
low_level_outputs.last_hidden_state.mean(dim=1)
high_level_outputs =
self.high_level_model(inputs_embeds=low_level_features.unsq
ueeze(1))
high_level_features =
high_level_outputs.last_hidden_state.mean(dim=1)
combined_features = torch.cat((high_level_features,
low_level_features), dim=1)
output = self.fc(combined_features)
return output
model = HierarchicalModel(high_level_model,
low_level_model)
