FAQ
Q: How much data can the system handle?
A: With basic setup: ~1 million rows comfortably. With distributed computing (Dask/Ray): ~100 million
rows. With GPU acceleration: ~10 million rows at high speed.
Q: What databases are supported?
A: PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQLite, MongoDB, Redis, Cassandra, Snow
flake, BigQuery, and any
SQLAlchemy-compatible database.
Q: Can I use this with real-time data?
A: Yes, configure with streaming sources:
